Shared-Residual World Models

In progress

A multimodal world model that splits each modality into a shared summary plus a modality-specific residual instead of forcing one aligned latent. Preliminary ALFRED results: better next-state prediction and native missing-modality inference, with no alignment loss.

Modality-Aware Fusion for AD Conversion Prediction

In progress

Predicting progression from MCI to Alzheimer's disease using ADNI imaging, PET, protein, genetic and clinical data. Our modality-aware fusion model matches the strongest tabular baseline on complete data and pulls significantly ahead once input modalities go missing — the setting it would actually be deployed under.
